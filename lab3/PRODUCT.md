# Travel MN

An offline Flutter travel catalogue for Mobile App Development Lab 3.
The governing brief is ../Лабораторийн ажил 3.pdf, including the screenshots on pages 9–10.

Users browse ten Mongolian destinations, read a short description, and see the region, approximate road distance from Ulaanbaatar, illustrative rating, and recommended season. Search and session-only favorites support browsing.

Target platforms: Android, iOS, and web. Material 3 follows the system light/dark setting. Compact widths use two grid columns, medium widths three, and widths of 840 dp or more use a 340 dp destination list beside the shared detail view.

Use local photographs and Roboto Slab fonts. No network service, authentication, maps, booking, or persistent storage is required for this lab.
